## Text

**Name:** Ashen Vigil

**App Store subtitle** (24/30): Horde Survival Roguelite

**Google Play short description** (66/80): Survive the undead horde, loot epic gear and slay colossal bosses.

**App Store keywords** (95/100): `survivor,roguelite,horde,dark fantasy,rpg,action,dungeon,knight,loot,boss,bullet heaven,offline`

**Full description:**

The last flame gutters beneath the cathedral. Hold it against the hollowed dead until the third bell tolls.

Ashen Vigil is a dark-fantasy horde survival roguelite. Move with one finger while your armaments strike on their own, roll through danger, and grow from a lone knight into a storm of steel, fire and lightning.

KEEP THE FLAME ALIVE
Your light is your lifeline. Feed the vigil flame or level up: every choice trades power for sight. Let it die and the dark comes for you.

PARRY THE DARK
Time your parry as a blow lands to stun the dead and fling their spells back. Bosses flash gold before a parryable strike: break their posture and strike while they stagger.

HOLD BACK THE HORDE
Hundreds of undead pour from the dark: hollows, plague rats, skeleton warriors, wraiths, cultists and dark knights. Every run is a fight to survive.

SLAY COLOSSAL BOSSES
Three bells toll in every stage. Face the Gaoler of Ash, the Rotting Mother and the Last Warden, and dodge their charges, slams and spirals.

BUILD YOUR LEGEND
Choose from 13 armaments, including one found only in each stage, and 7 relics as you level up. Carry up to four of each, so every pick matters. Max an armament and pair it with its relic to evolve it into something devastating.

LOOT EPIC GEAR
Open chests for helms, armor, rings and talismans in four rarities. Upgrade them, merge three into one of higher rarity, and chase legendaries. Drop odds and guarantees are always shown.

RAID DAILY DUNGEONS
Chase gilded thieves through the Gilded Vault, defend a sigil crystal in the Crystal Reliquary, slay the Den Mother in the Ember Hatchery to hatch a familiar companion, and survive the Trial of Bells boss rush.

SEASONAL EVENTS
Gather feathers from hordes and bosses to earn rare wings each season.

GROW STRONGER EVERY RUN
Earn gold and player levels, walk the talent path, unlock five origins and descend through four increasingly deadly stages.

No energy system. Play as long as you like.

**Category:** Action (Google Play). Games > Action, with Role Playing as the secondary category (App Store).

**Content rating answers to expect:** fantasy violence, blood (stylised), in-app purchases including random items (chests). There is no user-generated content and no chat.
